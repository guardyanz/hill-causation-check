# Hill Causation Scorecard

**Claim:** X → Y (write the claim as cause → outcome)

**Effect size:** RR / OR / HR with confidence interval, quoted exactly from the study.

**Study design:** RCT / cohort / case-control / cross-sectional / meta-analysis / case report.

**Population and setting:** Who was studied, where, and over what follow-up time.

**Source:** Full citation and URL of the study (not the headline). If the study could not be retrieved, write "Source not retrieved" and score every viewpoint Unknown.

## Notes

- Temporality is the only absolute viewpoint: if X did not precede Y, the verdict is NOT ESTABLISHED, whatever the other scores.
- Specificity is the weakest viewpoint: failing it alone never sinks a verdict.
- No numbers and no study design in the header → every score is Unknown, not guessed.
- This scorecard judges strength of evidence for a general claim. It does not give personal medical advice. Medical decisions belong with a qualified clinician.
